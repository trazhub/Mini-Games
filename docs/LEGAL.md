# Legal notes

These are design rules for the project, not legal advice. Get a lawyer's review before promoting the
site widely.

## Rules for games

1. Mechanics are free, expression is not. Use original art, synthesised sound and original level
   layouts.
2. Original names only. Never use the names or characters of existing commercial games or franchises,
   for example Counter-Strike, Minecraft, GTA, Among Us, Mini Militia, Ludo King, Tank Trouble,
   Feeding Frenzy, Tetris, Pac-Man, Mario, Pokemon, Angry Birds, Temple Run, Subway Surfers, Fruit
   Ninja, Clash, PUBG, Free Fire, 8 Ball Pool, WWE, Tekken, FIFA, Club Penguin, Ben 10, Papa's, Bloons,
   and similar. Use descriptive working titles instead ("Tank Duel", "Fish Feast", "Jet Squad",
   "Traitor Station").
3. Traditional games (Ludo, Chess, Checkers, Snakes & Ladders, pool rules, cricket rules, Mancala and
   similar) are free to implement. Only their branded editions are protected.
4. Held back until reviewed: platformers in the style of well-known franchises, falling-block
   stackers, maze-chase games, and anything that uses licensed characters.

## NOTICE

MiniGameHub contains original implementations of game ideas. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by,
or sponsored by the makers of any commercial game. All trademarks and product names mentioned
anywhere belong to their respective owners and are used only to describe what a game is similar to,
if at all.

If you believe something here infringes your rights, open a GitHub issue on this repository or, if
you prefer privacy, a private security advisory, and say what should change. It will be reviewed and
dealt with promptly.
